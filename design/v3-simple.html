Version 3 · rebuilt from scratch

Two words, one list, and nothing in the way

There's no tab bar and no cards. The big words Today and Goals at the top are the navigation. You add a task by typing at the bottom, where your thumb already is. Colour only appears when something needs your attention. All three phones below are live and share the same data.

Today
Type “Call dad sunday 7pm” at the bottom. Tap a task to edit it.
Goals · fits one screen
Tap + to log. Tap a goal for details.
New goal
Pick a type and the form changes

Everything you need, nothing more

Tasks with deadlinesToday, tomorrow, a weekday or next week. Times like 6pm work too.
Type it naturally“Text Sam friday 7pm” works out the day and time for you.
Repeating tasks“every sunday” or “daily”. When you finish one, the next one is added.
Late tasksShown in red at the top, with a one-tap button that moves them to tomorrow.
Edit, delete, undoTap a task to change it. After you tick one, you can undo it.
Four kinds of goalSave money, stay under a limit, do it often, or finish by a date.
On track or notEach goal says Ahead, On track, Behind, Slow down or Over limit.
One-tap loggingLog cigarettes and gym from Today or from Goals.
Year and quarterGoals finished each month, with totals for this year and this quarter.

Data is saved to your Google Drive, and the app works on phone and web. If this is the one, say “go” and I'll build it.
