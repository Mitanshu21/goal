Version 2 · still two tabs

Tasks and Goals, faster every day

The layout is the same as version 1. The changes make daily use quicker: type a task the way you'd say it, log a cigarette or a gym session straight from the home tab, and fix a late task with one tap. Both phones are live.

What's better than version 1

Type it naturallyType Call mom tomorrow 6pm and the app picks up the day and time by itself. every sunday or daily makes the task repeat.
Quick log on the home tabCigarettes and gym sit at the top of Tasks with a + button, so you don't need to open the Goals tab every time.
One tap on goal cardsThe goal cards on the Goals tab have the same + button. Goals that need attention move to the top.
Late tasks: “Tomorrow”Each overdue task has a button that moves it to tomorrow, so it doesn't sit in red.
Repeating tasksTick “Call mom · every Thu” and next week's call is added for you.
Undo and day progressA toast appears after each tick so you can undo it. A thin bar shows how much of today is done. The smoking goal also counts the days since your last cigarette.

Goals tab
Tap + on a card to log, or tap the card for details

Four kinds of goal, each with an on-track check

Save moneyA target amount and a date. The app compares what you've saved with what you should have saved by now, and tells you how much to save each month to finish on time.Buy a car · Buy a house
Stay under a limitAt most a set number per month. Log each one with a single tap. The app shows how many you have left and warns you if you're using them up too fast.Smoke at most 10 a month
Do it oftenA set number of times per week. Log each session. The app shows this week's progress and how many recent weeks you hit your target.Gym 4 times a week
Finish by a dateA deadline plus linked tasks. Progress is the share of those tasks that are done, compared with how much time has passed.Finish the React course
